Meet the Founder

Dontè Barry Dorlly

President & Founder — D.D. Sports Management Agency

FIFA Licensed
8+ Years Experience
3,000+ European Contacts
50+ Deals Closed

Born in Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal, and raised in the vibrant streets of Johannesburg, my football journey began on the dusty pitches where dreams are forged every day.

I pursued my passion as a player until 2017, when an injury sidelined me. Instead of stepping away, I chose to stay deeply involved — analysing matches with my teammates, diving into coaching books, and studying the game from a tactical and managerial perspective. That injury became the turning point that shifted my focus from playing to developing talent and understanding the business of football.

I transitioned into local coaching, dedicating myself to nurturing young South African players. During this time, I founded Thunder United F.C. — my first attempt at creating something bigger. Although the club didn't last long due to my limited business knowledge at the time, it taught me invaluable lessons and ignited a deeper hunger to grow.

Determined to do better, I invested heavily in my education — completing courses in Business Management, Negotiations, and Professional Athlete Management. These weren't just certificates. They were the foundation I needed to operate at the highest level.

Fast forward to today: I have built over 3,000 direct contacts across Europe — straight to club management and coaches, with no third parties or middlemen. This network was strengthened through trusted collaborations with high-profile players including Loris Karius (former Liverpool goalkeeper), Wesley França (Roma), Rayan Vitor (Bournemouth), Vladimir Coufal (former West Ham), and many other top prospects.

Every connection, every late-night call, and every relationship built represents years of persistence, integrity, and a genuine belief in South African talent.

My Vision

To be the most trusted bridge between exceptional South African football talent and the elite clubs and academies of Europe. I don't just move players — I build careers, protect futures, and create pathways that last beyond the pitch.

From the fields of Johannesburg to the floodlights of Europe, the journey continues.

Meet Our Financial Officer

Darren Cannavaro Kannemeyer

Financial Officer — D.D. Sports Management Agency

20+ Years in Banking
Treasury & Business Banking
Private Wealth Management

With more than 20 years of experience in the South African banking industry, I bring a wealth of financial knowledge, practical expertise and client-focused experience to my role as Financial Officer within the sport management industry. Throughout my banking career, I have developed a comprehensive understanding of financial services, treasury operations, business banking, customer relationship management and private wealth management. Each position has contributed to my understanding of financial operations, risk awareness, client service, financial responsibility and the importance of building lasting relationships based on trust, integrity and professionalism.

In my role as Financial Officer, I aim to apply this extensive experience to support the agency’s financial management, operational efficiency and long-term sustainability. I recognise that the sports industry presents unique financial opportunities and challenges, particularly for professional athletes whose earning potential, career longevity and financial circumstances can change significantly throughout their careers.

By combining my banking experience with an understanding of the financial needs of athletes and the business objectives of a sports management agency, I aim to add value through sound financial oversight, strategic thinking and a commitment to protecting and strengthening financial futures.

I am passionate about contributing to an environment where the agency can grow sustainably while its athletes are encouraged to build a solid financial foundation that extends beyond their playing careers. Through integrity, accountability and a client-first approach, I look forward to helping create lasting value for the agency and the people it represents.

My Focus

My focus is to contribute to a financially sound and well-managed agency while supporting a culture of responsible financial decision-making. I believe that financial success extends beyond immediate earnings; it requires careful planning, informed decisions and a long-term vision for sustainable wealth creation.
